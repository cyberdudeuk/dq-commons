Take a survey, run your own, or ask the Commons for help

Open to any organisation: central and local government, devolved administrations, the wider public sector, and voluntary and private bodies delivering public services. Surveys take 3 to 8 minutes. Results are shared openly as totals.

Surveys run on GitHub. Answer here, then press Continue on GitHub: a GitHub form opens with your answers already in it. GitHub issues are public and show your GitHub username, so answer for your organisation only if you're happy for that to be seen, or ask a colleague to submit for the team. Free-text answers stay with you and are never published. You can also download your answers for your own team.

Run your own survey through the Commons

  1. Write your questions in the same simple format the Commons uses (knowledge/surveys.yaml): single or multiple choice, ratings from 1 to 5, bands and free text.
  2. Check them against the rules: no names, emails or dates of birth; a privacy notice first; every question can be skipped; totals under 5 hidden.
  3. Send them as a pull request, or ask for help with the "Run a survey through the Commons" request.
  4. When it's approved, the survey appears here with its own GitHub form (made from the same file by scripts/gen_issue_forms.py), and its totals are counted every day for everyone to use.
