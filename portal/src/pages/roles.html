The delivery lifecycle: data quality at every phase and assurance gate

Phases follow the GDS Service Manual (discovery, alpha, beta, live). Gates follow the gateway reviews used for major projects, now led by the National Infrastructure and Service Transformation Authority (NISTA); departments run equivalent internal gates. Each phase ends with the question an assurance reviewer should ask.

Who does what (RACI)

R: responsible. A: accountable (one per activity). C: consulted. I: informed. Business-as-usual roles (owner, steward, quality lead) are included because programmes hand the data back to them.
