    How each step is placed

    Nothing on the map is a guess. Each step's position comes from the capability model by these rules, so it changes when the model does.

    Who does it (left to right)

    • People: a task done by a person, with no Commons tool.
    • Decision: a yes or no choice made by a person.
    • People with a tool: a person's task that a Commons tool supports.
    • The toolkit: a task the toolkit does automatically.

    How much it matters (bottom to top)

    • Required: the step cites the Data Asset Management policy or its mandatory guidance on critical data assets, metadata and ownership.
    • Recommended: the step cites other published guidance, such as the action plan guide, issues framework or Government Data Quality Framework.
    • Good practice: no guidance cited.
    • A decision takes the level of the most important step it leads to.

    Reading the corners

    Top left: high stakes and done by people, so keep human judgement and make it easy to evidence. Top right: high stakes and automated, so automate with checks and a person to review. Bottom right: let the toolkit do it. Bottom left: simplify with templates.

    Inspired by the Morgan Map, a way of mapping services by automation and criticality to trust.
