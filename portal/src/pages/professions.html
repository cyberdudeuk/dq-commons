The data quality capability set

Twelve sub-capabilities named by the Government Data Quality Framework and by open standards and bodies of knowledge (DAMA-DMBOK, the ISO 8000 series). Each card shows the anchor in each.

What we found

Data quality is everywhere and named almost nowhere. Most professions describe data quality skills under other names: quality assurance, data preparation, evidence, data standards. The Government Digital and Data framework has no standalone data quality skill. That makes the capability hard to see in job descriptions, skills audits and workforce plans.

Australia's APS Data Capability Framework is one model with a dedicated data quality area. The Civil Service Skills Taxonomy, now being adopted across professions, is a natural place to name data quality as a universal skill.
