Connectors, weaves and AI

How it works

1. Write your rules once

Describe your checks in the Commons rules format, or start from the rule library. Use the data quality check to test them on your own file first.

2. Weave them out

Pick your platform below. The weave writes the same rules in that platform's own format, ready to paste or load. Anything that won't translate is listed, never dropped quietly.

3. Connect your catalogue and AI

Use the directory to see each platform's open-source code, its MCP server and its AI agents for cataloguing and metadata, with links to check every claim.

Make rules for your platform

Everything runs in your browser. Nothing you paste leaves your device.

    AI agents and MCP for cataloguing and metadata
    
  
    The Model Context Protocol (MCP) is an open standard that lets AI assistants call tools in other systems. A catalogue's MCP server lets an assistant search assets, read owners and classifications, trace lineage and check quality, using the catalogue's own permissions.
