    Delivery objectives

    Tactical objectives cover the next two quarters. Strategic objectives set the direction of travel for the next few years. Each names the government strategy it supports and the backlog items that deliver it.

    Roadmap

    Now and Next are tactical; Later is strategic. Next fills in score order until our capacity for the quarter runs out; items with a dashed border wait for capacity. Choose an item to trace it.

    How we prioritise

    We score each item from 0 to 3 on four criteria, weight them, and adjust for effort (small items keep their full score; medium items 90%; large 80%). The score sets the priority: . Move the sliders to try your own weights: the Your order column shows how the order would change. Our published order uses our weights.

    Waiting for assessment

    Wishes people have suggested on GitHub that we haven't scored yet, most votes first. They are scored at the next monthly triage and either join the backlog or are closed with the reason.
