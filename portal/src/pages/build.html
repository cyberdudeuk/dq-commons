        Contact us

        Send us a message through this form. We never show the team's email address, which keeps spam and impersonation down. You don't need to give your name or email: we reply here, on this page, when you come back signed in.

        Up to 2,000 characters. Please don't include personal data, or anything official-sensitive.

        Security problem? Report it privately through GitHub's vulnerability reporting, not here.
