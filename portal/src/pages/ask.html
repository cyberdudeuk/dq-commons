Ask the Commons a question

Ask in plain English. The answer is drawn from every page, section and knowledge file in the Commons, with a link to where it came from, so you can check it and read more.

How it works: this is a search-based assistant, not generative AI. It finds the passages that best match your question and shows them word for word. It runs in your browser: your question is not sent anywhere or stored. Want to use your own organisation's AI assistant with the Commons? See AI assistants, MCP and connectors.

Tips for good answers

  • Use the words you would use with a colleague, for example "how do I prioritise issues".
  • Name the thing you care about: a framework, a role, a tool type, a policy.
  • Every answer links to its source. Check the source before you act.
