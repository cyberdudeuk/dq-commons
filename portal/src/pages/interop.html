Metadata Rosetta: one concept, eleven standards

61 cataloguing concepts mapped across DCAT v3, the UK Metadata Exchange Model, DCAT-AP and its geospatial and statistical profiles, DCAT-US, PROV-O, the Data Quality Vocabulary (DQV), and the three formats the Metadata Weaver also writes: schema.org, CKAN and ODCS. Use it to describe data the same way whichever catalogue it lands in. Quality concepts are marked, and each concept links to the standards in the standards catalogue that express it.

Standards catalogue: data, digital, cyber and technology

The standards used across UK government and the wider public sector, with their status: Mandated and Recommended are the GOV.UK open standards; UK guidance is required or expected by government guidance; Widely used are open or international standards in common use. Each links to its specification, the Rosetta concepts it expresses, whether the Weaver reads or writes it, and where to see it in public code.
