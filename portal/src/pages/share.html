Draft case studies

The kit drafts the GDS case study template from each asset's results. The steps taken, the before-and-after table and the reusable assets are filled in. People write the parts only they know: what triggered the work, the lessons, and whether it can be shared.

AssetTrust levelRules metDraft
  1. Open the draft and check every line against what really happened.
  2. Complete each "[To complete]" part: trigger, why you started there, actions, lessons.
  3. Choose a permission: publish as is, light redactions, anonymised, or GDS only.
  4. Send it to the GDS Data Management Team through their case study form.

No names or contact details are filled in. The three extra sections (CDA identifier, before and after, inclusion) are marked as proposed additions to the GDS template.

The tooling survey: fix before it launches

GDS's Cross-Government Data Quality Solutions Survey is well built: organisation questions once, then a repeating section per tool. Twelve small changes would let it answer the questions its own tooling review asks. Answers can't be re-sorted once collected, so these matter most before launch.

Analysing the answers

When the Microsoft Forms export arrives, one command turns it into one row per organisation and tool, with counts, averages and a small-numbers warning. Names, roles and emails are removed as the file is read and never written out.

python -m dqkit survey survey-export.xlsx --out survey-results
