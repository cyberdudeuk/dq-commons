Draft case studies

The kit drafts a case study from each asset's results. The steps taken, the before-and-after table and the reusable assets are filled in. People write the parts only they know: what triggered the work, the lessons, and whether it can be shared.

AssetTrust levelRules metDraft
  1. Open the draft and check every line against what really happened.
  2. Complete each "[To complete]" part: trigger, why you started there, actions, lessons.
  3. Choose a permission: publish as is, light redactions, anonymised, or internal only.
  4. Share it through the library, in Show and tell on GitHub Discussions (its form asks whether we may add it to the library), or your own community of practice.

No names or contact details are filled in. The three extra sections (CDA identifier, before and after, inclusion) are marked as proposed additions.
