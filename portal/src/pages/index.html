Who could this miss?

  • Data that leaves out groups of people can pass every overall check. The toolkit's inclusion checks look for unknown values and for quality that differs between groups.
  • People in Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland are often less well served by UK data. In the NDL pilot, 45 of 48 pages covering only part of the UK didn't point to data for the other nations.
  • Data without an open, machine-readable format is harder to use with assistive technology.
  • Staff who use assistive technology must be able to run checks and work the issue log. The tooling survey asks suppliers about this.

What this is, and isn't

  • A working prototype, built in the open as a public good: MIT-licensed code, OGL v3.0 content. It is not an official government product, and links to official guidance first.
  • Your data stays in your browser. Only what you choose to contribute is saved, and it is moderated.
  • Trust levels and AI drafts are suggestions for a person to confirm. They are not decisions.
