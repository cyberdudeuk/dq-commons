How the wheel surfaces in maturity

Each knowledge area shows up in the Commons maturity self-assessment themes and in the topics of the government's Data Maturity Assessment (DMA). Use this to read your maturity results as a capability map: a low theme points to the areas to strengthen. These mappings are the Commons' judgement.

One lifecycle for data and records

Data and records go through the same stages under different names. Running them as one lifecycle means retention, disposal and quality are decided together, and nothing a critical data asset depends on is lost.

Data and records frameworks: where they overlap

Records and information management has its own standards, methods and assessments. Many overlap with data management; using them together avoids duplicate work and fills gaps on both sides.
