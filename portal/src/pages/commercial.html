Govern · Buying and suppliers

Make suppliers responsible for the quality of public data

Most public sector data is created, held or changed by suppliers' systems. Without clear obligations, public bodies pay twice: once for the service, and again to find, fix and work around poor data. This page sets out the barriers, the principles, and model clauses, KPIs and tender questions that put the responsibility where it belongs.
