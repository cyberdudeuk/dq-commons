Do · Maturity self-assessment

How mature is your organisation's data quality practice?

For each of 8 themes, choose the statement closest to where you are today. You'll get a level for each theme, from Beginning to Mastering, and the next steps to move up. It takes about 10 minutes. Your answers stay in this browser.

Level names match the Data Maturity Assessment for Government so you can put the results side by side. The themes and statements are the Commons' own, written for data quality. It is a self-assessment aid, not an official DMA.

Each theme is a group of five options. Choose one per theme.
