How to use the Commons

You don't need to install anything or know how to code. Data you check stays in your browser. This guide covers the basics, every page, guided tours and common questions.

Quick start in 5 minutes

  1. On Home, choose your role or task.
  2. Press Take the tour at the top of any page for a walk-through. Escape leaves it.
  3. Open Check your data and run the example, or open your own CSV file. (The Python toolkit also reads Excel, ODS, JSON, Parquet, SQL databases and CKAN catalogues.)
  4. Read each rule's pass rate against its target, and the priority of anything that fails.
  5. Save the rules file so you can run the same checks next month, or in the pipeline.

Guided tours

Each link opens the page and starts its tour. Tours move focus to a small panel next to the part being explained. Use Next and Back, or the arrow keys, and Escape to stop.

Common tasks

Check a dataset

Go to Check your data. Open a CSV file or choose an example. Add rules from the rule builder, or start from the rule library: person, address, organisation, geography and publication templates. Press Run the checks.

Identifier checks test the format and, where there is one, the check digit: NHS number, National Insurance number, postcode, UPRN, GSS code, Companies House number, charity number, school URN, ODS code and LEI.

Find what a framework or policy expects

Go to Frameworks and standards. Filter by nation, sector or type. Open a framework to see its dimensions and how they map. The clause table breaks policies down line by line, with devolved and international comparisons and known gaps.

Assess your maturity

Go to Maturity self-assessment. For each of 8 themes, choose the statement that fits best. You'll get a level per theme and next steps. Your answers stay in your browser.

Prioritise issues

Use the calculator on Issues framework. Enter how important the purpose is and how bad the impact would be. It gives the target, the priority band and how quickly to fix.

Get value from AI safely

Read AI for data quality, then try the rules assistant, which runs in your browser with no AI service. Always review what it drafts: the page tests every draft against the kit before showing it.

Contribute, take a survey, or ask for help

Use Community for the contributions library and wishlist, and Surveys and commissions to take or run a survey or request help. If saving isn't available where you opened the portal, each form links to a GitHub form instead.

Automate checks every week

See Operating model and Pipeline status. The GitHub Actions pipeline measures every asset, keeps history, opens escalation issues and rebuilds this portal.

Questions

Is this official government guidance?

No. It's a prototype public good service. It links to official sources first and marks anything not yet verified.

Does my data get uploaded?

No. Checks run in your browser. Only things you choose to submit (a contribution, a survey answer, a request) are saved, and those never include your name or email unless you add a contact detail.

Is it only for the National Data Library or the DAM policy?

No. Those are two of the scenarios. The toolkit works for any data, and the Scenarios page covers statistics, health, local government, geospatial, personal data, AI and more.

Can we use the toolkit with our existing tools?

Yes. Rules are plain YAML and results are W3C DQV, CSV and JSON. See Tooling for routes into Great Expectations, R validate, Purview, Fabric and others, and the MCP server for AI agents.

How do I report a problem?

See the support tiers on Operating model. Accessibility problems are answered within 1 working day.

Accessibility

We aim to meet WCAG 2.2 AA. The portal works with a keyboard alone, screen readers, voice control and zoom up to 400%, follows your light or dark setting and respects reduced motion. Charts always have a table or text alternative.

Known gaps: the portal hasn't yet had an independent audit (planned, XDMH-213), and there is no Welsh version yet (XDMH-216). Tell us about anything that doesn't work for you through Surveys and commissions or the service desk.

Privacy

  • Data you check is processed in your browser and is not sent anywhere.
  • Surveys never ask for names or emails. Results are shown only as totals, and totals under 5 are hidden.
  • Contributions and requests are moderated before anything is public.
  • The maturity self-assessment is kept only in your browser.
