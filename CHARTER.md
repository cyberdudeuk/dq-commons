# Co-design and contribution charter

_Generated from `knowledge/build.yaml` by `scripts/gen_community_docs.py`. Edit the YAML, not this file._

We build the Commons with the people who use it, so it meets real needs, includes everyone and stays a public good.

## Principles

- **Open by default.** Code is MIT, content is OGL v3.0. Decisions, roadmap and backlog are public.
- **Nothing about us without us.** People affected by data, including people who are often left out, help shape what we build.
- **Plain English.** We write for everyone. Jargon is explained or removed.
- **Accessible to all.** We aim for WCAG 2.2 AA, test with assistive technology, and offer alternatives for every activity.
- **Privacy first.** We never need your name or email. Contributions credit organisations, or nobody.
- **Evidence over opinion.** Claims link to sources. Unchecked claims are labelled.
- **Fair participation.** Sessions rotate times and formats, so different time zones, caring responsibilities and working patterns can take part.
- **Transparent decisions.** Votes inform priority; the product owner decides and records why in the decision log.

## House rules

- Be respectful and constructive. Follow the code of conduct.
- No personal data: no names, emails, phone numbers or identifiers of real people, unless it's your own organisation's public contact route.
- No official-sensitive or classified material. If in doubt, leave it out.
- No advertising or selling. Suppliers are welcome to share evidence and open resources, not sales material.
- Cite your sources and say what you haven't checked.
- Only share what you have the right to share, under an open licence.
- AI-assisted content is welcome if you say so and a person has checked it.

## Guidelines

- Describe the problem and who has it before suggesting a solution.
- Keep contributions short. Link to longer documents.
- Use the issue forms on GitHub, or the forms here, so we have what we need.
- One idea per suggestion, so people can vote on it.
- Say which nation, sector or jurisdiction your contribution applies to.

## Safeguards

- **Moderation.** Every contribution is checked before it's public: no personal data, accurate links, plain English, an open licence.
- **Automatic personal data checks.** Forms block obvious emails, phone numbers, National Insurance and NHS numbers before anything is sent.
- **Small numbers hidden.** Survey and benchmark results show totals only, and hide groups under 5.
- **No tracking.** No analytics, cookies or third-party trackers. Fonts are the only external request.
- **Masked contact.** Contact is through a form. The team's email address is never shown, to prevent spam and impersonation.
- **Security reports in private.** Security problems go through GitHub private vulnerability reporting, never public issues.
- **Right to withdraw.** Ask through the contact form and we'll remove your contribution.
- **Safeguarding.** Content about vulnerable people must be about data and services, never about identifiable individuals.
- **Conflicts of interest.** Contributors say if they work for a supplier of a product they're discussing.
- **Escalation.** Concerns about conduct or content go to the moderators through the contact form; they reply within 5 working days.

## How we co-design

| Method | When | How |
|---|---|---|
| Discovery interviews | Before we build something new | 45-minute conversations about your work, not about the Commons. |
| Co-design workshops | Each quarter | Small groups shape a feature together. Times rotate across time zones. |
| Usability testing | Before each release | Try a task while we watch and learn. Assistive technology users are always included. |
| Asynchronous feedback | Any time | Start or comment on an idea in GitHub Discussions (Ideas category, with its own form), or use the contact form, at your own pace. |
| Show and tells | Monthly | We show what changed and why, with a written summary in the GitHub Release notes and a Show and tell discussion for questions. Recordings, if any, have captions and a transcript and are kept outside the repository. |

## Decisions

Votes and co-design inform priorities. The product owner role decides and records the reason in `docs/DECISIONS.md`.
