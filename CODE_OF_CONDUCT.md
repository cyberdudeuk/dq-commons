# Code of conduct

The Public Sector Data Quality Commons is for everyone who works with public sector data, in the UK, the Crown
Dependencies, the Overseas Territories, the Commonwealth and beyond. We want it to be welcoming, safe and useful.

This code is based on the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1, adapted for a
public sector community. It applies on GitHub, in the portal, in co-design sessions and anywhere you represent the Commons.

## What we expect

- Be kind, patient and respectful, including when you disagree.
- Welcome people of every background, identity, ability, nation, sector and level of experience.
- Write in plain English and explain jargon, so everyone can take part.
- Give and accept constructive feedback about the work, not the person.
- Respect people's privacy: never share anyone's personal data, including your own colleagues'.
- Respect each jurisdiction: don't assume one country's laws or frameworks apply everywhere.
- Say when you have a conflict of interest, such as working for a supplier of a product being discussed.

## What isn't acceptable

- Harassment, insults, discrimination or unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Publishing anyone's private information without their explicit permission.
- Sharing official-sensitive, classified or confidential material.
- Advertising, spam, or using the Commons to sell.
- Anything that would make the Commons unsafe for someone to take part.

## Reporting a problem

Use the contact form on the portal's **Help us build this** page (choose "Report a problem with content" or
"Something else"). You don't need to give your name or email. Moderators reply within 5 working days. For security
problems, use GitHub's private vulnerability reporting (see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md)).

## What happens next

Moderators may remove content, ask someone to change their behaviour, or remove someone from the community. We'll
explain our decision unless doing so would put someone at risk. Decisions about the code itself are recorded in
`docs/DECISIONS.md`.
