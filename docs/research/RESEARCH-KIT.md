# User research kit: five organisation types (BL-01, issue #1)

Everything needed to run the research in `docs/USER-RESEARCH-BUNDLE.md` ("Research plan for v1.0.0"). It is
ready to use; the sessions need people, so the issue stays open until they have run.

## 1. Who to recruit (screener)

Aim for 10 to 12 people. Quotas are minimums, not targets to hit exactly.

| Quota | Minimum |
|---|---|
| Central department | 2 |
| Local authority (at least one outside England) | 2 |
| Devolved government body, Crown Dependency or Overseas Territory | 2 |
| NHS or other health and care body | 1 |
| Voluntary or community sector organisation | 2 |
| People who use assistive technology (screen reader, magnification, voice, switch) | 2 |
| People with little experience of data tools | 3 |
| Welsh speakers | 1 |
| People working outside London and the South East | 5 |
| Mix of ages, sexes, ethnic groups and disabilities | spread across the sample; recorded only as totals, never against a person |

Screener questions (in a form that stores no names until a session is booked):
1. What kind of organisation do you work for? (list above, or "other")
2. Which nation or territory do you work in?
3. How often do you work with data in spreadsheets or databases? (never, sometimes, often)
4. Do you use any assistive technology? (no, yes: which, prefer not to say)
5. What would make taking part easier? (time of day, captions, British Sign Language, breaks, Welsh, paper copies, other)

## 2. Consent (read out and shared in advance, in plain English)

- Taking part is voluntary. You can stop at any time without saying why.
- We will take notes. We will record the session only if you agree, and delete the recording after 30 days.
- We never publish your name, your organisation's name (unless you ask us to) or anything that could identify you.
- Findings are reported as themes and totals.
- You can withdraw your answers within 30 days using the contact form ("Withdraw my contribution").

## 3. Session guide (60 minutes)

| Minutes | Part |
|---|---|
| 5 | Welcome, consent, access needs check |
| 10 | Context: your role, the data you look after, what "good data" means where you work |
| 35 | Tasks (pick 4): find the frameworks that apply to you; check the example CSV with an identifier rule; complete the maturity self-assessment; draft rules with the assistant and say whether you trust them; find a page with search and the "On this page" drawer; read a page's "In short" summary and say if it helps; contribute a resource through the form |
| 10 | Reflection: what would stop you using or contributing to the Commons? Does "Commons" feel like it is for you? What is missing for your nation or sector? |

For each task record: completed (yes, with help, no), time, words used, where people hesitated. Don't help unless asked
or stuck for more than 2 minutes, then note it.

## 4. Accessible arrangements

Offer: sessions at different times of day (see `docs/community/CO-DESIGN.md` for time zones), captions on video calls,
a British Sign Language interpreter on request, materials in advance, a phone option, breaks, and Welsh-language
sessions with a Welsh-speaking facilitator. Pay for interpreters from the research budget, never ask participants to arrange them.

## 5. Synthesis template

| Theme | Evidence (session codes, never names) | How many sessions | Severity (blocks, slows, annoys) | Backlog item |
|---|---|---|---|---|

Write up within 2 weeks: findings, updated personas, the top 5 changes, and anything that excludes a group (fix those first).
Publish the summary on the Commons; keep notes for 6 months, then delete.
