**Subject:** Two small corrections to the Data Quality Issues Framework tables

Hello,

While building an open tool that applies the data quality issues framework, I found two points in
v1.0 that may still be in use in departments.

1. **Tables 7 and 8, 97.5% target.** The High band start is printed as 96.75%. The stated rule ("halfway
   between the Middle band start point and the target") gives 96.25%. A department using the printed
   figure would put 96.5% in the Middle band rather than the High band, which doubles its priority score.
2. **Band score labels.** The list reads "1 – Low, 2 – Middle, 3 – High", but the worked example gives
   score 1 to performance just below the target (the upper band). The 2026 GOV.UK page is clear on
   this; the v1.0 wording could be aligned or withdrawn.

The comparison, with other points about step numbering and ESDA/CDA terminology, is in the attached
guidance crosswalk.
